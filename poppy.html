Poppy 2.0 - What's New
Phase 4: Better Builds
  • Automated build, test and fix loop in hidden phone iframe.
  • Design review from mobile, tablet, and desktop screenshots.
  • Multi-file projects (.html, .css, .js) with ZIP export & import.
  • Offline image Assets library with DALL-E & Stability generation.
  • 12 Starter templates, 6 style packs, daily token cap & evals.
Phase 5: Smarter Sites
  • Autonomous Agent mode with DOM interaction, testing & store inspection.
  • Embedded SQLite database (poppy.db) with migrations & export.
  • Poppy Relay for CORS on Notion, Stripe, Resend & Slack.
  • poppy.room peer-to-peer WebRTC & channel sync.
  • Background automations on site open, data change, and timer.
  • Supabase auth and realtime integration.
Phase 6: Ship & Share
  • One-click publishing to GitHub Pages, Netlify, Vercel & Cloudflare.
  • Full Library with folders, tags, search, pinned favorites & version diffs.
  • Encrypted backups with WebCrypto AES-GCM & Gist sync.
  • Security Center with permissions manager, connector log & panic wipe.
  • Multi-language support (English, Hindi, Spanish, French) & high contrast.
